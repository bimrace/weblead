Transportation buildings & facilities

What makes transportation buildings different

Large volumes change the ventilation problem

A concourse or terminal hall is a single tall space, not a stack of floors. Ventilation, smoke extraction and comfort cooling have to work across long spans, and the ductwork that does it often runs at high level along the roof structure. Coordinating that against long-span steel and roof drainage is where the effort concentrates.

Buildings that never close

Stations and terminals operate for most of the day, every day. Maintenance has to happen around passengers, so access to plant, valves and dampers is a design requirement. We test access and maintenance clearances as part of coordination, not only hard clashes.

A clear line between building and infrastructure

Transportation projects combine buildings with specialist infrastructure. Our scope is the building services inside and serving the building. Track, signalling, roads, bridges, tunnels and airside systems need their own specialists, and we say so at the start.

How an engagement runs

Five steps from enquiry to handover

The same sequence on every project, so you know at each point what you will receive next and what we need from you.

01

Enquiry and inputs

You send the scope, stage and the drawings or models you have. We confirm what is usable, what is missing and which services the project actually needs.

02

Scope and proposal

We agree the deliverables, the level of model detail (LOD/LOI — how much geometry and data each element carries), file formats, programme and what is excluded.

03

Modelling and coordination

Revit models are built from your design information and checked against architecture, structure and each other. AI-assisted checking groups the issues; engineers resolve them.

04

QA/QC and issue

Every issue passes model QA/QC before it leaves us. Drawings, schedules, quantities and clash reports are issued under revision control.

05

Handover

Native models, drawings, registers and a handover note listing files, assumptions and anything left open — so the building owner can actually use what they receive.

Scope boundary

What is excluded on transportation projects

Stated at enquiry stage, because a boundary discovered in month three is a dispute and the same boundary stated now is just information.

  • Rail alignments, track systems, roads, bridges, tunnels and other civil infrastructure are excluded unless separately agreed and supported by the appropriate specialists.
  • Signalling, communications, traction power and airside/aviation systems are specialist engineering scopes and are excluded unless separately agreed.
  • Specialist smoke-control analysis, tunnel ventilation and fire engineering require agreed design responsibility and qualified specialists.
  • We do not claim specialist airport, railway or metro credentials. Statutory and operator approvals remain with the appointed designers.

FAQ

Questions we are asked at enquiry stage

Do you work on airport terminals and railway stations?

On their building services — HVAC, ventilation, plumbing, drainage, fire protection and electrical — yes, where the project's specialist requirements are confirmed. We do not work on track, signalling, roads, tunnels or airside systems.

What is the difference between a transportation building and transportation infrastructure?

The building is the terminal, station, depot or car park and the services inside it. The infrastructure is the track, roads, bridges, tunnels and systems such as signalling. Our scope is the building services; infrastructure needs its own specialists.

Can you coordinate our MEP models with the wider project's infrastructure models?

We can federate building-services models with the models the wider team supplies and report clashes at the interfaces. Resolving infrastructure design remains with its designers.

Do you model smoke extraction systems?

We model and coordinate smoke extract ductwork and equipment to the design supplied. Smoke control analysis and tunnel ventilation design are specialist scopes we do not provide unless separately agreed with qualified specialists.

Do you have airport or railway project credentials?

We do not publish project history until a client releases it, and we do not claim sector credentials we have not verified. Ask at enquiry stage and we will show our workflow and QA output under NDA.
