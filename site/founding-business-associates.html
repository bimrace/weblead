01 / About the opportunity

A business-building opportunity, not a Director appointment.

BIM RACE is seeking founding business associates—not additional Directors. Associates contribute financially and professionally to the organization's establishment, operations, business development and project delivery.

The aim is to build a professionally managed organization with a strong technical team, reliable business operations, domestic and international project opportunities, and a sustainable business model. Contributions are discussed and documented before any commitment is made.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

Business Associate status does not automatically provide company shareholding or Directorship.

Any future equity participation, if considered, requires separate legal documentation and applicable corporate approvals.
